#### The Ground Breaking Lenire Device for Tinnitus Treatment
Tinnitus—the perception of sound without an external source—affects millions of people worldwide and can be a deeply frustrating condition. Columbia Basin Hearing Center is proud to offer Lenire , an innovative, FDA-approved tinnitus treatment that uses a unique bimodal neuromodulation approach to help reduce tinnitus symptoms.
Lenire has been recommended by 83% of patients who have completed treatment and has demonstrated high efficacy across three large FDA-reviewed clinical trials . These studies show meaningful, clinically significant improvements for a majority of patients, offering real hope where few effective options previously existed.

## WHAT IS LENIRE?
![An infographic explaining the Lenire device, a dual stimulation device designed to treat tinnitus. It features images of three components: a controller, a tonguetip device, and headphones, with descriptive text about each item's function.](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6627c873af0c127944582e9e/cf1ce7b3-4ad2-4746-b279-c90ca61e9ab8/What+is+the+Lenire+Device.png)
Lenire uses a combination of sound therapy delivered through Bluetooth headphones and a tongue-tip stimulator to provide gentle electrical stimulation. Together, this simultaneous stimulation alters or reduces tinnitus pathways over time.

#### Safe
Lenire is the only bimodal neuromodulation device with FDA approval following a controlled trial. In recent studies, nearly 80% of participants experienced a meaningful reduction in tinnitus severity.
