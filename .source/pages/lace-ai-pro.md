## WHAT IS LACE AI?
![A person holding a smartphone displaying a health and speech therapy app with options for speech in noise, speech reading, rapid speech, and working memory.](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6627c873af0c127944582e9e/0707e1b4-a623-40d6-adac-373622364570/6764d14a1d681295dc5aecfa_LAIP+-+General+Mockup.png)
LACE AI (Listening and Communication Enhancement) is an AI-powered auditory training program proven to strengthen the brain’s ability to understand speech. Hearing aids amplify sound — but your brain does the listening. LACE AI helps retrain the brain to interpret speech more clearly, especially in noise.
