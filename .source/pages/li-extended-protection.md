- Hearing aids issued by L&I only include one year of loss and damage coverage .

- If you lose your hearing aids after this period , L&I will not replace them .

L&I Policy Reminder :
> L&I will pay for repairs and replacement due to normal wear and tear. Any other loss or damage, such as those from non-work accidents, is the worker’s responsibility. L&I does not cover hearing aids that are lost, stolen, or damaged in non-work-related incidents.
