# Audinell Ultra Powerful Drying Capsules
A 2 pack of extra drying capsules / tablets for the Audinell Dry Cup. Gives you 6 months total (3 months per capsule) of additional dehumidification for your dry cup.
Each capsule measures 63mm and is specifically designed to fit in the Audinell Dry Cup. When used together with the dry cup, the capsules help remove sweat & moisture from Airpods, hearing aids, and other hearing and listening devices. Our translucent capsules are packaged in hermetically sealed, individual packets & contain highly absorbent orange silica gel. When your desiccant changes from its original bright orange color to more of a pale yellow, it is time to replace the desiccant. Each desiccant should get you approximately 3 months' of use, or 6 months total for this 2 pack.
